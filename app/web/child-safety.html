Bua Family is a private app for one family: its family tree, news, photos, events and support for each other. Only relatives an admin has approved can open it. This page sets out our published standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), what is not allowed, how to report a concern, and who to contact.

Our standards

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and any sexual content involving a minor are strictly prohibited on Bua Family, and any account that shares, requests or promotes it is removed.

The following are not allowed anywhere in the app — in profiles, moments, photos, comments, events, messages, mentorship conversations, elders' stories or welfare fund records:

  • Sexual content of any kind involving a person under 18, including images, video, audio, drawings and text.
  • Grooming, or trying to build a sexual relationship with a minor.
  • Sextortion, blackmail or threats using intimate images.
  • Asking for, offering, sharing or linking to child sexual abuse material.
  • Using the app to contact a child for sexual purposes, or to arrange to meet a child for that purpose.
  • Trafficking, or any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers a minor.

Who the app is for

The app is not made for children and is not directed at them. It is intended for adults (18 and over) in one family. Every account must be approved by a family admin before it can be used, and the app is limited to that family — it is not open to the public.

How to report a concern

  • In the app: every moment, photo, comment, profile and private message has a Report option (in its menu, or by holding a message). The member chooses a reason — Child safety concern is the first — and can add a note. Reports are private: only the family's admins see them, and a copy of what was reported is kept with the report even if it is later deleted. Admins are notified at once, and a child safety report is sent to them as urgent.
  • Remove or block: from a report, admins can remove the content, suspend the account (which ends its access immediately) or delete it. Any member can block another member, so that neither can message the other. Members can also delete their own account at any time.
  • By email: for any child safety concern, including a concern about a member, write to ahmadbaba560@gmail.com. This is the designated point of contact for child safety, and is monitored.

What we do about it

  • Remove the content and remove the account from the app.
  • Preserve the relevant records so they can be given to the authorities.
  • Report apparent child sexual abuse material to the relevant authorities and child protection bodies, as required by law, without notifying the person who posted it.
  • Cooperate fully with law enforcement and with the relevant national and regional authorities in any investigation.

Reporting to the authorities

Where a report concerns a child in Nigeria, we report it to the National Agency for the Prohibition of Trafficking in Persons (NAPTIP) and to the Nigeria Police Force. Where the child or the person involved is elsewhere, we report it to the relevant national authority in that country. Reports involving child sexual abuse material are also made to the appropriate international reporting bodies.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or local police first.

Our commitment

We comply with all relevant child safety laws in the countries where the app is available, and we report child sexual abuse material and child sexual exploitation to regional and national authorities as the law requires. We review these standards as the app and the law change.

Changes

If these standards change, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Wannan shafin ya bayyana ka'idojinmu game da cin zarafin yara da lalata da su (CSAE), abin da ba a yarda da shi ba, yadda za a ba da rahoton wata damuwa, da wanda za a tuntuɓa.

Ka'idojinmu

Ba mu yarda da cin zarafin yara da lalata da su ko kaɗan ba. Hotunan lalata da yara (CSAM) da duk wani abu na lalata da ya shafi ƙarami haramun ne a Bua Family, kuma duk asusun da ya yada, ya nema ko ya ƙarfafa shi, za a cire shi.

Waɗannan ba a yarda da su a ko'ina cikin manhajar ba — a bayanan mutum, labarai, hotuna, sharhi, taruka, saƙonni, tattaunawar jagoranci, labaran dattawa ko lissafin asusun taimako:

  • Duk wani abu na lalata da ya shafi mutum ƙasa da shekara 18, ciki har da hotuna, bidiyo, murya, zane da rubutu.
  • Lalata ko ƙoƙarin kulla dangantaka ta lalata da ƙarami.
  • Tsoratarwa ko tsinuwa da hotunan sirri (sextortion).
  • Neman, bayarwa, yadawa ko nuna hanyar haɗi zuwa hotunan lalata da yara.
  • Yin amfani da manhajar don tuntuɓar yaro don lalata, ko shirya haɗuwa da yaro don haka.
  • Fataucin mutane, ko duk wani abu da ke lalata, cin zarafi ko jefa ƙarami cikin haɗari.

Wanda manhajar ta ke don su

Ba don yara aka yi manhajar ba, kuma ba a nufa ta gare su ba. Don manya ne (shekara 18 zuwa sama) a cikin iyali ɗaya. Dole shugaban iyali ya amince da kowane asusu kafin a iya amfani da shi, kuma manhajar ta iyali ɗaya ce kawai — ba a buɗe ta ga jama'a ba.

Yadda za a ba da rahoton damuwa

  • A cikin manhajar: kowane labari, hoto, sharhi, bayanin mutum da saƙon sirri yana da zaɓin Kai rahoto (a cikin menu ɗinsa, ko ta danna saƙo ka riƙe). Ɗan uwa zai zaɓi dalili — Damuwa kan lafiyar yara shi ne na farko — kuma zai iya ƙara bayani. Rahoto sirri ne: shugabannin iyali ne kaɗai ke gani, kuma ana ajiye kwafin abin da aka kai rahotonsa ko da an goge shi daga baya. Ana sanar da shugabanni nan take, kuma rahoton lafiyar yara yana zuwa musu a matsayin gaggawa.
  • Cirewa ko toshewa: daga rahoto, shugabanni na iya cire abin, dakatar da asusun (wanda ke kawo ƙarshen damarsa nan take) ko goge shi. Kowane ɗan uwa na iya toshe wani, don kada ɗayansu ya iya aika wa ɗayan saƙo. ’Yan uwa kuma na iya goge asusunsu a kowane lokaci.
  • Ta imel: don duk wata damuwa kan lafiyar yara, ciki har da damuwa kan wani memba, a rubuta zuwa ahmadbaba560@gmail.com. Wannan shi ne wanda aka nada don lafiyar yara, kuma ana kula da shi.

Abin da muke yi game da shi

  • Cire abin da aka wallafa da cire asusun daga manhajar.
  • Ajiye bayanan da suka dace domin a iya ba da su ga hukumomi.
  • Ba da rahoton abin da ya nuna hotunan lalata da yara ga hukumomi da ƙungiyoyin kare yara da suka dace, kamar yadda doka ta ce, ba tare da sanar da wanda ya wallafa ba.
  • Haɗin kai da jami'an tsaro da hukumomin ƙasa da na yanki a kowane bincike.

Ba da rahoto ga hukumomi

Idan rahoton ya shafi yaro a Najeriya, muna kai shi ga Hukumar Yaki da Fataucin Mutane (NAPTIP) da ’Yan Sandan Najeriya. Idan yaron ko wanda abin ya shafa na wata ƙasa ne, muna kai shi ga hukumar da ta dace a wannan ƙasa. Ana kuma kai rahoton hotunan lalata da yara ga ƙungiyoyin da suka dace na ƙasa da ƙasa.

Idan yaro na cikin haɗari nan take, tuntuɓi jami'an gaggawa ko ’yan sanda na yankin da farko.

Alƙawarinmu
