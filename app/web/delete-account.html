Bua Family appManhajar Bua Family

In the app (straight away)

  1. Open Bua Family and sign in.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Tap Delete my account, read what is removed, tick the box and confirm.

The family's only admin has to make someone else an admin first.

Without the app

Send a request from the email address or phone number you sign in with, saying you want your Bua Family account deleted, to a family admin. It is done within 30 days, and you are told when it is.

What is deleted

Your account (sign-in, phone number and email) and what you shared: moments, comments, likes, photos you uploaded, memories, mentorship messages, requests, replies to events, votes, notifications and your devices.

What stays, without your name

Records that belong to the whole family: your place in the family tree, albums and events you created (with everyone else's photos and replies), polls, elders' stories you recorded, and welfare fund payments (the fund's accounts must still add up). To change or remove your entry in the family tree, ask a family admin.

Read the full privacy policy.

A cikin manhajar (nan take)

  1. Buɗe Bua Family ka shiga.
  2. Je zuwa Ƙari.
  3. Taɓa Goge asusuna, karanta abin da za a cire, yi alama a akwatin ka tabbatar.

Idan kai kaɗai ne shugaban iyali, sai ka mai da wani shugaba tukuna.

Ba tare da manhajar ba

Aika buƙata daga imel ko lambar wayar da kake shiga da ita, cewa kana so a goge asusunka na Bua Family, zuwa ga shugaban iyali. Za a yi cikin kwana 30, kuma za a sanar da kai.

Abin da za a goge

Asusunka (shiga, lambar waya da imel) da abin da ka raba: labarai, sharhi, so, hotunan da ka ɗora, tunawa, saƙonnin jagoranci, buƙatu, amsoshin taruka, ƙuri’u, sanarwa da na’urorinka.

Abin da zai zauna, ba tare da sunanka ba

Bayanan da na iyali ne gaba ɗaya: matsayinka a bishiyar iyali, kundin hotuna da taruka da ka ƙirƙira (tare da hotuna da amsoshin wasu), ƙuri’u, labaran dattawa da ka naɗa, da gudummawar asusun taimako (lissafin asusun dole ya daidaita). Don canza ko cire sunanka a bishiyar iyali, tambayi shugaban iyali.

Karanta cikakkun manufofin sirri.
