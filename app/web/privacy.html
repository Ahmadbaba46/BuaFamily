Bua Family is a private app for one family: its family tree, news, photos, events and support for each other. Only family members an admin has approved can open it. This page explains what the app keeps, why, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

Who runs it

The app is made by the developer for the family. Family admins approve members and look after the family's information. Contact: a family admin, through the app.

What the app keeps

  • Your account: your name, email address or phone number, and your Google name and email if you sign in with Google. Passwords are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • The family tree: names, relationships, dates and places of birth and death, photos, life stories, education, work, skills and contact details. Health details (blood group, genotype) are private unless the person chooses to share them.
  • What you share: moments, photos, comments, likes, events and replies, polls and votes, memories of relatives who have died, mentorship messages, blood requests and offers, and welfare fund payments with their receipts.
  • Elders' stories: voice recordings that admins make with an elder's permission. The microphone is used only while recording.
  • How the app is used: sign-ins, which pages are opened and when, the kind of device (Android or web) and the app version, so admins can see who is active and keep the app working. Page visits are kept for 90 days and the rest of this log for a year.
  • Notifications: a notification token for your device, and your phone number if you choose to get SMS.

The app does not collect your location, your phone's contacts or advertising IDs. There are no ads, and the family's information is never sold.

Who can see it

  • Approved family members see the family tree and what is shared with the family.
  • Admins and the welfare fund committee also see what they need to run the family's affairs: who has joined, payments, and the activity log.
  • Health details are seen only by the person, admins, and whoever the person shares them with.
  • Private mentorship conversations are seen only by the two people in them.

Services the app uses

  • Supabase: stores the information and photos, and handles sign-in.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivers notifications.
  • Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Termii: sends SMS to Nigerian phone numbers, when the family has SMS turned on.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.

They process information only to provide these services. Everything travels encrypted (HTTPS), and access rules check every request.

Deleting your account

In the app, open More → Delete my account. Or ask for it on the account deletion page. Your sign-in, phone and email, and what you shared (moments, comments, likes, photos you uploaded, memories, messages and requests) are deleted. Records that belong to the whole family stay without your name: your place in the family tree, albums and events you created, polls, elders' stories you recorded, and welfare fund payments (the fund's accounts must still add up).

Children

The app is not made for children. Children's names and dates in the family tree are added by their parents or relatives and are seen only by approved family members.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

  • Bishiyar iyali: sunaye, dangantaka, ranaku da wuraren haihuwa da rasuwa, hotuna, tarihin rayuwa, karatu, aiki, ƙwarewa da hanyoyin tuntuɓa. Bayanan lafiya (rukunin jini, genotype) sirri ne sai mutum ya zaɓi ya raba.

  • Shugabanni da kwamitin asusun taimako suna ganin abin da suke buƙata don gudanar da al’amuran iyali: waɗanda suka shigo, biyan kuɗi, da rajistar ayyuka.

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: isar da sanarwa.

Goge asusunka

A cikin manhajar, buɗe Ƙari → Goge asusuna. Ko ka nema a shafin goge asusu. Za a goge shigarka, lambar waya da imel, da abin da ka raba (labarai, sharhi, so, hotunan da ka ɗora, tunawa, saƙonni da buƙatu). Bayanan da na iyali ne gaba ɗaya za su zauna ba tare da sunanka ba: matsayinka a bishiyar iyali, kundin hotuna da taruka da ka ƙirƙira, ƙuri’u, labaran dattawa da ka naɗa, da gudummawar asusun taimako (lissafin asusun dole ya daidaita).
